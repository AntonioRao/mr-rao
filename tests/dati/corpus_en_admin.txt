PROCUREMENT REVIEW — QUARTERLY REPORT
Reference: PO-2024-118 / Annex B
Issued: March 2025

1. SCOPE

This report covers the procurement activity of the Data Centre and
Network Services group for the period ending 31 March. It supersedes the
report issued in August and should be read alongside the Service Level
Agreement and the Bill of Materials attached at Annex C.

2. SUPPLIERS UNDER REVIEW

Sterling Systems Ltd continues to hold the primary hosting contract.
Baker & Price Limited supplies rack hardware under a framework agreement
renewed in May. Rose Analytics provides the reporting layer. Whitfield
Holdings remains on the approved list but has not been engaged since
June.

Green Lane Logistics handles physical media destruction. The collection
point is the loading bay on Church Road, with a secondary site at the
Sterling Way depot in Reading. A third site in Bath was closed in
January and has not been replaced.

3. GOVERNANCE

Responsibility sits with the Head of Procurement, reporting to the Chief
Information Security Officer. The Data Protection Officer reviews every
contract that involves processing on behalf of the group. The Project
Manager for the migration workstream chairs the weekly call.

The Bill introduced last session does not change these obligations. The
grace period for existing contracts runs to the end of the financial
year.

4. STANDARDS AND CONTROLS

Controls are mapped to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A, with particular
attention to Clause 8.3 and Clause 5.19. Supplier assessments follow
NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5. The internal quality mark is applied only
after the evidence pack has been signed off.

Mark the register entry as closed once the assessment is filed. Long
lead times on the optical modules remain the principal risk; costs rose
by nine per cent against the forecast.
